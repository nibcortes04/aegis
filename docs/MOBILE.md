# 📱 Google Antigravity Remote Control — Android Official PWA Guide

This guide details how to install, configure, and pair the official **Google Antigravity Remote Control Progressive Web App (PWA)** on Android smartphones and tablets for seamless agent supervision on the go.

---

## 🌟 Why the Official PWA?

Google Antigravity distributes its mobile companion as an official **Progressive Web App (PWA)** hosted at **[https://antigravity.google](https://antigravity.google)** rather than a legacy APK on Google Play Store.

### Key Benefits:
- **Zero Third-Party Downloads:** No APK sideloading or untrusted binaries required.
- **Enterprise Security:** Authenticated directly through your Google Account with TLS 1.3 encryption.
- **Standalone Native Experience:** Runs in fullscreen mode with splash screen, app icon, and no browser URL bar.
- **Remote Two-Factor Approvals:** Review and approve or reject critical agent actions (`rm -rf`, `git push`, VPS docker commands) directly from your smartphone while away from your desk.
- **Web Push Notifications:** Receive instant notifications when your agent completes a complex turn or requests clarification.

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Installation on Android

### Step 1: Open Google Chrome on your Android Device
Open **Google Chrome** (or any modern Chromium-based browser like Brave or Samsung Internet).

### Step 2: Navigate to the Official Pairing Portal
Go to:
```text
https://antigravity.google
```
*(Tip: You can also scan the QR code generated by running `aegis mobile --pair` on your terminal).*

### Step 3: Sign In with your Google Account
Sign in using the **exact same Google Account** associated with your Antigravity CLI (`agy`) or Antigravity IDE on your workstation or VPS.

### Step 4: Install the App to your Home Screen
1. Tap the three-dot menu (**`⋮`**) in the top-right corner of Chrome.
2. Select **"Install app"** (or **"Add to Home screen"**).
3. Confirm the prompt: **"Install Antigravity"**.
4. The Antigravity icon will now appear on your home screen and in your Android app drawer.

## 🖥️ Workstation / VPS Daemon Setup

Before your Android PWA can receive session telemetry and send approvals, start the local remote-control daemon on your host machine:

### 1. Start the Remote Control Daemon
```bash
agy remote-control start
```

### 2. Verify Daemon Health
```bash
agy remote-control status
```
*Sample Output:*
```text
● agy-remote-control.service - Antigravity Remote Control Daemon
   Status: Active (Running)
   Connected: Yes (https://antigravity.google)
   Active Sessions: 2
```

### 3. Stop the Daemon
```bash
agy remote-control stop
```

## 🔐 Security & Remote Approvals Architecture

When your agent operates in `workspace-safe`, `full-developer`, or `vps-production` mode:
1. **Critical Actions Intercepted:** When the Two-Factor Safety Gate intercepts a dangerous command, a notification pops up on your Android phone.
2. **Review Diff & Rationale:** The mobile UI displays the tool name, target files, and command line.
3. **One-Tap Decision:** Tap **"Approve"** or **"Deny"**. The decision is routed back sub-100ms through Google's secure gateway to your workstation's AGY runtime.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Is there an APK on Google Play Store?
**No.** Google Antigravity Remote Control is exclusively engineered as an official PWA. PWAs receive automatic updates from Google without requiring Play Store downloads.

### Q2: Do I need to open firewall ports or setup SSH port forwarding?
**No.** The connection between your host and `antigravity.google` is initiated outbound by the `agy remote-control` daemon over secure WebSockets (WSS). No inbound ports need to be opened on your router or VPS.

### Q3: How do I enable sound or push notifications?
When first opening the PWA in Chrome, tap **"Allow"** when prompted for notification permissions.
